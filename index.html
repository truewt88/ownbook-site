Why people use it

Budget what you have

How it works

Three steps to a budget you trust.

Bring in your transactions

Drop in the CSV statements your bank already gives you. Supported banks are read automatically; for any other bank, you teach the app the layout once.

Place every egg

Money that's arrived waits, ready to place. Give each part a place (groceries, rent, a savings goal) and the app keeps the maths straight as the month moves.

Check in, anywhere

See this month against your usual, what's left to spend, and how your nest is growing, on the desktop or your phone.
