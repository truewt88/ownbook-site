Privacy Policy

Ownbook is made by an independent developer in Singapore. It was built so your money records stay with you, and this page says exactly what that means.

The short version

  • No account. You never sign up or sign in to use Ownbook.
  • Your budget, transactions, accounts and settings live in a folder on your own device, or in your own cloud storage if you choose.
  • We never receive, see or store your financial data.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no selling of data.

Your data

Everything you enter or import is saved as files in a data folder you pick. Backups are saved there too. If you put that folder in Dropbox, Google Drive or OneDrive so your phone and computer share it, that service stores the files under your own account and its own privacy policy. Ownbook only reads and writes the folder; we have no access to it.

Deleting the folder deletes your data. Uninstalling the app leaves the folder where it is.

When the app goes online

Ownbook works offline. It connects to the internet only in these cases:

  • Share prices. If you track investments, the app asks Yahoo Finance for prices of the symbols you hold (for example D05.SI). Only the symbols are sent, never your amounts or other data.
  • Update check (Windows version downloaded from this website). The app checks for a newer version. Versions from the Microsoft Store and Google Play are updated by the store instead.
  • Optional AI help with statement layouts. Only if you add your own Anthropic API key in Settings and choose to use it: the first rows of the statement file you are setting up are sent to Anthropic to work out its columns. This is off unless you turn it on.

The services above see your device's IP address, as with any internet request.

This website

This website sets no cookies and loads nothing from third parties. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

If you email us

If you write to us, we keep your email address and message only to reply and to fix what you reported. We don't add you to any list and don't share it.

Your choices

Because your financial data never reaches us, there is nothing for us to hand over or delete: it's in your folder, under your control. To have your emails with us deleted, or for any privacy question, write to privacy@useownbook.com. We follow Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will show the new date at the top. Anything that would send more of your data anywhere would be told to you in the app first.
