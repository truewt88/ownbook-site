Android: join the test

Your budget on the go. Use it alone, or share the desktop app's data folder through your cloud storage. Works offline.

  1. Join the tester group: groups.google.com/g/ownbook-testers. Use the same Google account as your phone's Play Store.
  2. Open the testing page and tap Become a tester.
  3. Install Ownbook from the Google Play link on that page. Updates come through Google Play.
Become a tester

Android 7 or newer. Google asks new apps to keep testers for 14 days before the public launch, so please keep it installed that long. See what it does in the Android guide.
